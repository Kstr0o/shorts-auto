Day 16. Toni becomes a tour guide. In his own neighborhood. He owes 240 dollars.
The tour costs ten dollars. Highlights: the bakery, the bus stop and a very old tree.
Two tourists sign up. They are his grandparents.
Stop one: the bakery. Toni says it was founded in fourteen fifty. It opened in two thousand nine.
Stop two: the bus stop. Toni says a famous king once waited here. Mostly people wait for the 14.
Stop three: the tree. He says it is a thousand years old. A kid says it was planted last year.
Grandma laughs so much she cries. Grandpa pays double.
Day 16 balance. He makes twenty, plus a twenty dollar tip. Toni owes 200 dollars.
What business should he try tomorrow? Comment it, and Toni will do it.
