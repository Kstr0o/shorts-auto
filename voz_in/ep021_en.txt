Day 12. Toni starts a dog walking business. He owes 314 dollars. And he does not own a dog.
He charges five dollars a walk. Five neighbors say yes.
Five dogs. One leash each. Toni has two hands.
A cat appears. All five dogs run at once. Toni is dragged down the street on his belly.
He crashes into a trash can, and the dogs knock over a hot dog cart.
The hot dog man is angry. The dogs are not. They are very happy.
Toni pays for the hot dogs. Twenty dollars.
Day 12 balance. He makes twenty five, he spends twenty. Toni owes 309 dollars. And his shirt is ruined.
What business should he try tomorrow? Comment it, and Toni will do it.
