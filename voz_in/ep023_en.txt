Day 14. Toni opens a lemonade stand. In the middle of winter. He owes 310 dollars.
He wears a puffer jacket. The lemonade is frozen solid.
A customer arrives. Toni sells her a lemonade ice cube for two dollars.
She tries to bite it. Her tooth says no.
Another customer wants hot lemonade. Toni has an idea. He puts the cup on the radiator.
It works. Hot lemonade, four dollars a cup. People love it.
Fifteen customers line up. Toni is rich for ten minutes. Then the lemons run out.
Day 14 balance. He makes sixty, he spends fifteen on lemons. Toni owes 265 dollars. His first big win.
What business should he try tomorrow? Comment it, and Toni will do it.
