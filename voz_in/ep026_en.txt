Day 17. Toni sells autographs. He is not famous. He owes 200 dollars.
He makes a sign: Autographs, five dollars. Limited edition. Also unlimited edition.
A kid buys one. It says: To my best friend, Toni.
Word spreads. Twelve people line up outside the school. Nobody knows who he is.
A man asks: are you a singer? Toni says maybe. Are you an actor? Toni says maybe.
Toni signs sixty autographs. His hand cramps. His signature is now a squiggle.
Then someone asks him to sign a hot dog bun. Toni says yes.
Day 17 balance. He makes sixty, he spends eight on pens. Toni owes 148 dollars.
What business should he try tomorrow? Comment it, and Toni will do it.
