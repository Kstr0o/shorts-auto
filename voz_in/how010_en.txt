Why does every airplane window have a tiny hole at the bottom?
A plane window is actually made of three panes.
The outer pane takes the pressure difference between the cabin and the thin air outside.
The middle pane is a backup, in case the outer one ever fails.
The tiny hole is in that middle pane. It's called a bleed hole.
It lets air flow into the gap, so the outer pane carries the load, not the middle one.
It also lets moisture escape, so your window doesn't fog up.
The inner pane is just a plastic shield that protects the others from scratches.
That little hole is why you can see the clouds so clearly.
