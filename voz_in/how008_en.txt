Why are manhole covers always round?
A square cover can fall straight into the hole.
Turn it diagonally, and it slips right through.
A square's diagonal is longer than its sides.
A circle is the same width in every direction.
So no matter how you turn it, it can never fall in.
It also rolls easily, and it needs no alignment when you put it back.
One cover can weigh over fifty kilos, so rolling beats lifting.
Simple shapes solve big problems.
