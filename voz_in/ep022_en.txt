Day 13. Toni becomes a street magician. He owes 309 dollars. And he knows zero tricks.
He buys a top hat for six dollars. It has a hole in it.
First trick: pulling a rabbit out of the hat. He does not have a rabbit.
He pulls out a sock. The crowd leaves. One kid stays and asks if it's clean.
Second trick: making a coin disappear. It works perfectly.
The coin fell through the hole in the hat. And now he can't find it.
A grandma applauds. She says it is the best trick she has seen all year. She tips him five dollars.
