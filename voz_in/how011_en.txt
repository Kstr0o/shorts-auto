Why do some power lines have giant colored balls on them?
They're called marker balls, and they're there for planes.
Cables are thin and almost invisible, especially to helicopters and crop dusters.
The balls are big and bright, usually orange, white or red.
They go where lines cross valleys, rivers or roads, where aircraft fly low.
Each ball is about the size of a beach ball, made of fiberglass or plastic.
You'll also find them near airports and helipads.
Some even light up at night.
A cheap ball can save a pilot's life.
