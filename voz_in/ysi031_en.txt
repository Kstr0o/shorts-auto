What if every volcano on Earth erupted at once?
About thirteen hundred and fifty volcanoes have erupted in the last eleven thousand years.
Right now around forty are erupting, and almost nobody notices.
If they all blew together, the ash would block out the sky.
Sunlight would not reach the ground, and the Earth would cool for years.
A small version happened in eighteen fifteen, with the volcano Tambora.
The next year had no summer. Crops failed and half the world went hungry.
And that was just one volcano.
