Day 11. Toni opens a restaurant. In his kitchen. He owes 314 dollars.
The menu has one dish. Mom's lasagna. The price is fifteen dollars.
Mom does not know. She thinks Toni is just hungry.
Two neighbors book a table. They bring their own chairs.
Toni wears a bow tie made of a sock. He calls himself Chef Antonio.
He serves the lasagna. A neighbor asks for a fork. Toni only has spoons.
Then mom walks in and sees two strangers eating her dinner.
She makes Toni refund both neighbors. Thirty dollars gone. And he washes every plate.
Day 11 balance. He makes thirty, he refunds thirty. Toni still owes 314 dollars. And his hands smell like soap.
What business should he try tomorrow? Comment it, and Toni will do it.
