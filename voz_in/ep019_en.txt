Day 10. Toni teaches skydiving. He has never jumped. And he has no plane.
Toni owes 332 dollars. So he charges ten per lesson. Up front.
Three students sign up. One of them took his get rich course on day 2. She learned nothing.
The plane is his bed. Jump altitude: sixteen inches. The parachute is a bedsheet.
Advanced level: jumping off the wardrobe. Toni climbs up to show them. And finds out he is scared of heights.
So a student jumps instead. He lands on the bed. And the bed snaps in half.
His mom hears it from the kitchen. Fixing the bed: twelve dollars. Toni pays.
Day 10 balance. He makes thirty, he spends twelve. Toni owes 314 dollars. And tonight he sleeps on the floor.
