# Structure (scaled to length)

Beats as percentages of runtime. Multiply by chosen length. Narration ~150 words/min.

| Beat | % of runtime | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Cold open | 0-6% | Concrete image + open question. No intro. |
| 2. Rules, through action | 6-18% | Viewer learns the power by watching it used. No lecture. |
| 3. The dream (honeymoon) | 18-35% | Fun montage of 2-3 uses. Show the appeal honestly. |
| 4. The turn | 35-58% | First crack. The power doesn't solve things or has a second rule. |
| 5. The human scene | 58-85% | One extended scene with a specific person and dialogue. Emotional center. |
| 6. Decision + cliffhanger | 85-100% | Cut before the choice. One quiet line. Part 2 hook. |

Example for 10 minutes: hook 0:00-0:35, rules 0:35-1:50, dream 1:50-3:30, turn 3:30-5:45, human scene 5:45-8:30, ending 8:30-10:00.

## Short formats (under 6 minutes)

Merge beats 2 and 3. Keep the human scene at 25% minimum. Never cut the hidden cost.

## Series logic (3 parts)

- Part 1: premise, dream, first cost, cliffhanger.
- Part 2: the cost escalates; the character tries to fix it and fails.
- Part 3: a choice that costs something real. Bittersweet, not tidy.

## Retention rhythm

Every 30-45 seconds there should be a new question, reversal, reveal or image. Mark them. If a stretch has none, add one or cut it.
