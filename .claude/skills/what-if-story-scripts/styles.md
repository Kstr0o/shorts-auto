# Styles

Apply the chosen style on top of the shared structure and style guide.

## 1. Tech "What If"
- Setting: near-future or present day; a technology that almost exists.
- Tone: calm, precise, quietly unsettling. Ground in how the technology plausibly works.
- Cost: loss of privacy, skill, trust, or human connection.
- Visuals: screens, interfaces, notifications, empty rooms.
- Ending: a small, human consequence rather than a robot uprising.

## 2. Superpower "What If" (Ifverse style)
- Setting: ordinary life plus one impossible ability.
- Tone: starts playful, turns heavy. "Amazing... and awful."
- Cost: the power isolates, costs time, or hurts someone close.
- Ending: a choice left open, a Part 2 hook.

## 3. Survival / extreme scenario
- Tone: urgent, practical, rule-driven. Ticking clocks and resource limits.
- Cost: physical limits and hard moral choices.
- Structure tweak: rules and mechanics get more time, but still shown through action.
- Ending: survival with a price, or a final escalation.

## 4. Emotional drama
- Tone: slow, intimate, specific. Fewer plot beats, longer scenes.
- Cost: relationships, memory, regret.
- Structure tweak: human scene starts earlier and runs longer (up to 40% of runtime).
- Ending: quiet, bittersweet, no twist needed.

## 5. Dark / thriller twist
- Tone: tense, minimal, short sentences. Details that feel slightly wrong.
- Cost: safety, trust, sanity.
- Structure tweak: plant a clue in the first minute that pays off in the last.
- Ending: a shock reveal that reframes the opening line.

## 6. Something else
Ask the user to describe the tone, setting and ending feel in one sentence, then adapt the closest style above.
