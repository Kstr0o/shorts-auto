# Hook Checklist

A hook is the first 15-45 seconds. It must contain a concrete image and an open question. Score every hook 0-5 (one point each).

1. **Immersion**: places the viewer inside a scene with "you" in present tense.
2. **Contrast**: sets a mundane modern thing against an unsettling reality.
3. **Stakes or ticking clock**: a consequence, deadline or constraint within the first 30 seconds.
4. **Subversion**: challenges an assumption ("you'd want this" -> "this is where it goes wrong").
5. **Single thesis question**: one clear premise the whole video answers.

## Hook types

- **Specific image**: a number above a head, a phone showing an impossible battery life.
- **Contradiction**: "It sounds amazing. It's also awful."
- **Ticking clock**: a countdown, a deadline, a number.
- **Stakes question**: a question the viewer cannot answer without watching.

## Rules

- No greetings, channel intro, or "in this video."
- The first line must work with the screen black.
- Hooks under ~60 words. Short sentences. One idea per line.
- Hook promises must be paid off in the video. Never bait.
- Generate 10, recommend 2, explain why in one line each.
