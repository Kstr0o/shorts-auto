# Idea Engine

## What makes an idea good for this niche

Score each candidate 1-5 on each criterion. Only recommend ideas scoring 4+ on the first three.

1. **Hidden cost clarity** - can the cost be said in one sentence?
2. **Human stake** - is there a specific person with something to lose?
3. **Visual fit** - can animation show it in one image (a number, a glow, an empty room)?
4. **Timeliness** - does it connect to something people are talking about now (new tech, new research, news, cultural anxiety)?
5. **Low saturation** - few strong existing videos on this exact angle.
6. **Series potential** - can it carry 3 parts with escalating cost?

## Finding timely angles

Look for tension points people are anxious or curious about right now:
- AI and automation (what changes when machines do X)
- Biology and longevity (new research on aging, sleep, memory)
- Neuroscience and psychology (attention, memory, emotion)
- Surveillance and privacy
- Space and climate
- Social media and identity
- Everyday-tech edge cases (what if the thing everyone relies on stopped working)

Search queries: "[topic] new study [current year]", "[topic] debate", "what if [premise]" for saturation. Pick a headline or discovery, then ask: *what would living with this be like for one person?*

## Making a common idea niche

Common ideas (immortality, time travel, mind reading, super speed, no sleep) are saturated. Make them niche by:
- **Narrowing the power**: not "read minds", but "hear only what people almost said."
- **Adding a constraint**: it works only once a day, only on people you love, only for the next 30 days.
- **Changing whose cost it is**: the power affects the people around you, not you.
- **Attaching it to a current event**: an AI, a drug, or a technology that now exists in a small form.
- **Choosing an ordinary setting**: dinner table, commute, hospital waiting room.

## Candidate format

For each of 5 candidates:
- **Title** (curiosity, not clickbait)
- **One-line promise**
- **Hidden cost**
- **Who pays** (a character type)
- **Why now** (the signal found)
- **Saturation note** (what you found, honestly)
- **Scores** (6 criteria)

Recommend one. Do not claim an idea is "untouched" without evidence.
